Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our cakes, baking process, or deliveries? Find answers here!

Are your cakes 100% vegetarian?
Yes! Only 100% pure vegetarian cakes are made here.
Who bakes these cakes?
Shital Pramod Koli is the baker and owner of this business, and she herself bakes fresh and delicious cakes with love.
What kinds of cakes can we get?
You can get different types of delicious cakes such as Chocolate, Pineapple, Truffle, Dark Chocolate, Black Forest, Red Velvet, Rasmalai, Blueberry, and more.
What categories of cakes do you have?
We offer designer and customized cakes across categories like Birthday, Wedding, Anniversary, Engagement, and special Celebrations.
Where can we get the cakes delivered? Are deliveries available?
Home delivery is available only within a 5 KM radius from our shop. If you live farther away, you can place your order online and pick up your cake by visiting our shop directly.
Who is eligible to place an online order?
Currently, online booking and delivery services are exclusively for people living in Dhule.
How do I place an order?
Simply browse our gallery, select your favorite cake, choose your preferred weight and customizations, add it to your cart, and proceed to checkout and payment.
Can I customize a cake design or message?
Yes! You can add personalized text messages to be written on the cake and provide special instructions for design tweaks or specific theme requests.
How does the payment process work?
We make checkout easy through direct UPI scanning (via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) followed by email notification so we can instantly confirm your payment.
How will I know when my order is confirmed?
After payment and notification, our team will review your order and call you to confirm your delivery address and exact delivery time. You can also track your order status in real time under "Order History".
Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?
If you need to make urgent changes or cancellations, please contact us immediately over phone or email before preparation begins.
Do you offer multi-tier cakes for grand weddings?
Yes, we specialize in gorgeous 2-tier, 3-tier, and wedding engagement cakes crafted to make your grand events unforgettable.
Are your cakes freshly baked for each order?
Absolutely! Every single cake is baked fresh using premium ingredients and fresh cream right after your order is confirmed.
How should I store my cake after delivery?
Fresh cream and fruit cakes should be kept refrigerated and consumed within 24–48 hours for the best taste and texture. Bring them to room temperature for 10–15 minutes before serving.
How can I contact customer support?
You can reach out to us through the Contact page on our website or email us directly at orders@jiyascake.com for any inquiries.
